# Getting Support with Crewloom

Welcome to **Crewloom**! We offer multiple support channels tailored for community developers, production teams, and enterprise organizations.

---

## 1. Community & Open-Source Support

For general questions, bug reports, and feature suggestions:

- **GitHub Issues:**  
  Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue in the relevant repository:
  - [Crewloom Toolkit Issues](https://github.com/crewloom/crewloom/issues)
  - [Bridge Control Room Issues](https://github.com/crewloom/bridge/issues)
- **GitHub Discussions:**  
  Ask architectural questions, share setups, or discuss role designs.
- **Documentation:**  
  Check repository READMEs and the `docs/` directories for quick-start guides and contract specifications.

---

## 2. Enterprise & Commercial Support (by Rumuze)

For companies running mission-critical agent fleets, requiring dedicated support, or needing custom integrations:

### Services Offered
- **Enterprise SLA Support:** Guaranteed response times and hotfix delivery for production systems.
- **Custom Agent Roles & Private Skills:** Engineering tailored specialist roles trained on your internal workflows.
- **On-Premise / VPC Deployments:** Assistance with enterprise security hardening, air-gapped environments, and custom telemetry.
- **Architectural Consulting:** Pair programming and code audits for agentic systems and multi-agent control rooms.
